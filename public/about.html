About Me!

I am a retired Front-end Architect from Chennai, India.

I love books, art, music and movies. I used to be a <a href="https://www.deviantart.com/rajasegar">Graphic Designer</a> and Systems Programmer once.</p> I love programming languages like <a href="https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript">Javascript</a>, <a href="https://www.ruby-lang.org/en/">Ruby</a>, <a href="https://www.haskell.org/">Haskell</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisp_(programming_language)">Lisp</a>. I wholeheartedly embrace Functional Programming, Code clarity, Performance, Accessibility and Internationalization.</p> I am passionate about Open Source Software and Contributions, recently, I have been writing tools in Script-Fu (A dialect of Scheme) for GIMP</p> My favorite editors are <a href="https://www.gnu.org/software/emacs/">Emacs</a> and <a href="https://www.vim.org/">Vim</a> (in order of preference)</p>
